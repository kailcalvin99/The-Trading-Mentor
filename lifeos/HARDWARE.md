# LifeOS Hardware Concept

The first LifeOS hardware concept is an Android-based Digital Audio Player designed for intentional living, AI assistance, and reduced distraction.

## Core Concept

The device would use Android as a practical foundation while limiting the experience to focused LifeOS workflows. It should provide the flexibility of an existing mobile operating system without recreating the attention-heavy patterns of a standard smartphone.

## Key Features

- Android-based Digital Audio Player foundation
- Physical buttons for fast, tactile control
- Small secondary display for glanceable status and focused interactions
- Minimal notifications to reduce interruption and attention fragmentation
- AI voice assistant for planning, capture, reminders, reflection, and quick answers
- Long battery life for dependable daily use
- Audio-first interaction for moments when looking at a screen is unnecessary

## Design Philosophy

The hardware should support intentional living instead of endless scrolling. It should make it easy to capture thoughts, review priorities, speak with the AI Chief of Staff, and stay aligned with meaningful goals.

The device should be useful throughout the day without becoming another source of distraction. Every hardware and software decision should reinforce calm, focus, and purposeful action.
