# LifeOS Vision

LifeOS is envisioned as a true personal operating system: a unified layer for managing the important parts of a person's life with clarity, focus, and intentionality.

## AI Chief of Staff

At the center of LifeOS is an AI Chief of Staff that helps the user plan, prioritize, remember, reflect, and act. Rather than behaving like a generic chatbot, the assistant should understand the user's goals, routines, preferences, responsibilities, and current context.

Its role is to help reduce mental clutter, surface what matters, and support better execution across daily life.

## Unified Life Dashboard

LifeOS should eventually provide a unified dashboard across the major areas of life, including:

- Health
- Money
- Relationships
- Work
- Learning
- Goals
- Projects

The dashboard should make it easier to see the state of each area, identify tradeoffs, and make intentional decisions without requiring the user to jump between many disconnected tools.

## Minimal-Distraction Philosophy

LifeOS should be designed around minimal distraction. The product should not compete for attention with endless feeds, unnecessary notifications, or addictive engagement loops.

The experience should be calm, focused, and supportive. Every feature should help the user live more intentionally rather than spend more time inside software.

## Future Dedicated Device

Over time, LifeOS may expand beyond web and mobile apps into a dedicated LifeOS device. This device would be designed around intentional use, quick access to the AI assistant, long battery life, and reduced exposure to distracting apps.

The device should feel like a life companion rather than another attention-consuming screen.

## Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal is for LifeOS to become a true personal operating system: a practical, trusted, and intelligent layer that helps people organize their lives, make better decisions, and stay aligned with what matters most.
