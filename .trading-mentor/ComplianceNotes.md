# Compliance and Trust Notes

## Purpose

This file captures risk guardrails. It is not legal advice.

Alex should get qualified legal review before scaling paid financial education, AI mentor behavior, testimonials, affiliate offers, or performance-related claims.

## Current Guardrail

The Trading Mentor should be positioned as education, planning, journaling, risk management, and review.

Avoid positioning it as:

- financial advice
- investment advice
- trading signals
- guaranteed profitability
- managed trading
- copy trading

## Public Claims Checklist

Before shipping marketing copy, pricing copy, onboarding, testimonials, AI prompts, or SEO pages, check:

- Does this imply guaranteed profit?
- Does this imply typical user results without evidence?
- Does this present hypothetical results clearly and cautiously?
- Does this pressure the user to trade?
- Does this sound like personalized advice?
- Does this explain risk plainly?
- Does this preserve education-first positioning?

## AI Mentor Checklist

The AI mentor should:

- explain concepts
- ask risk questions
- point back to the user's rules
- calculate position size from user-provided inputs
- encourage no-trade decisions when rules are not met
- clearly avoid personalized buy/sell instructions

The AI mentor should not:

- tell the user to buy or sell
- predict profit
- guarantee win rate
- claim certainty
- give personalized trade recommendations
- create urgency around entering a market

## Source Notes For Review

Official sources worth reviewing during legal/compliance hardening:

- CFTC advisory on commodity trading systems sold on the internet.
- NFA promotional material rules and hypothetical performance guidance.
- SEC/IARD and Investor.gov investment adviser registration information.

These sources reinforce the need to avoid misleading performance claims, clearly treat hypothetical results as limited, and understand when paid advice can create registration issues.
