# The Trading Mentor Business Plan

## North Star

The Trading Mentor becomes the trusted AI-assisted trading education desk for self-directed ICT/SMC futures traders who want discipline, risk control, journaling, and structured learning.

## Positioning

The product is not:

- a signal room
- a copy-trading service
- a profit guarantee
- a gambling app

The product is:

- an education system
- a planning desk
- a discipline coach
- a risk review layer
- a smart journal
- a structured academy

## Primary Customer

Self-directed futures traders learning ICT/SMC concepts, especially NQ/MNQ traders who struggle with consistency, risk control, journaling, and emotional discipline.

## Offer

Free:

- beginner academy access
- daily planner
- limited AI mentor usage
- basic risk/journal preview

Standard:

- full academy
- risk shield
- unlimited AI mentor
- stronger planning/review flow

Premium:

- smart journal
- analytics
- advanced review
- TradingView/webhook-related features if legally and technically safe

## Value Proposition

Review charts. Build discipline. Protect risk.

The user pays because the product helps them act less impulsively and review their process more clearly.

## First Business Milestones

### Milestone 1: Trustworthy Product Foundation

- Move off Replit.
- Verify auth, payments, database, AI, and domain.
- Remove secret and deployment risk.
- Audit public claims.

### Milestone 2: Activation

The first session should get the user to:

- understand the product promise
- create an account
- complete a planner or academy step
- see one useful AI mentor interaction
- understand the paid upgrade

### Milestone 3: Retention

The first week should get the user to:

- complete multiple planner days
- log or review trades
- see discipline patterns
- return to academy
- understand risk shield value

### Milestone 4: Revenue

Only scale acquisition after:

- checkout works
- subscriptions update correctly
- support/refund path exists
- trust-safe copy is reviewed
- onboarding converts at least some organic traffic

## Growth Channels

Highest fit:

- founder-led YouTube/TikTok content about discipline and journaling
- SEO pages around trading journal, ICT mentor, risk management, prop firm risk tracking
- demo clips showing planner, journal, and AI review
- small trader communities with education-first positioning
- email capture from free academy lessons

Avoid early:

- paid ads before conversion is proven
- affiliate promises before compliance review
- income-claim marketing
- "live callout" style content

## Metrics

Track:

- visitor to signup conversion
- signup to first planner completion
- signup to first journal entry
- signup to first AI mentor message
- free to paid conversion
- paid churn
- failed checkout rate
- support/refund reasons

Do not optimize for:

- number of trades taken
- daily PnL excitement
- streaks that encourage overtrading

## First 30-Day Operating Plan

Week 1:

- Complete off-Replit local baseline.
- Choose hosting/database target.
- Rotate exposed keys.
- Create staging plan.

Week 2:

- Implement portable config.
- Deploy staging.
- Verify auth, pricing, Stripe, database, and AI behavior.

Week 3:

- Cut over production domain if staging passes.
- Audit marketing and AI boundaries.
- Fix launch-blocking bugs.

Week 4:

- Tighten onboarding.
- Tighten pricing.
