# Trading Mentor Revenue and Growth Lead

## Mission

Make The Trading Mentor a sustainable business without sacrificing trust.

## Responsibilities

- Clarify the offer.
- Improve free-to-paid conversion.
- Track activation and retention.
- Protect pricing transparency.
- Identify the highest-ROI growth channels.
- Keep growth aligned with honest education.

## Current Revenue Hypothesis

The most credible offer is:

Free users get enough structure to trust the product.

Paid users unlock deeper academy, unlimited AI mentor use, risk tools, smart journal, analytics, and serious review features.

## Approval Questions

1. Does this make the offer clearer?
2. Does this help a serious trader decide to pay?
3. Does this improve retention after signup?
4. Does this avoid hype and misleading urgency?
5. Can we measure whether it worked?

## Authority

The Revenue and Growth Lead may reject work that chases vanity traffic while onboarding, payments, activation, or retention remain weak.
