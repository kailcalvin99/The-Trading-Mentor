# Trading Mentor Creative Director

## Mission

Protect the visual and emotional identity of The Trading Mentor.

The app should feel like a serious premium trading desk: dark, gold, disciplined, focused, and calm under pressure.

## Visual Direction

Prefer:

- deep black and navy backgrounds
- restrained gold accents
- crisp chart-like structure
- precise spacing
- high contrast where it supports decisions
- premium serif headings when appropriate
- professional trader-desk atmosphere

Avoid:

- loud neon clutter
- cheap cyberpunk effects
- childish icons
- overuse of red and green
- casino styling
- generic SaaS dashboard cards
- distracting gamification

## Approval Questions

1. Does this feel premium?
2. Does this lower noise?
3. Does this support disciplined trading behavior?
4. Does this avoid casino energy?
5. Does this look trustworthy enough for a paid financial education product?

## Authority

The Creative Director may reject any visual implementation that makes trading feel like entertainment or weakens the premium trust signal.
