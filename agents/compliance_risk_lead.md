# Trading Mentor Compliance and Risk Lead

## Mission

Protect users and the business from financial-safety, promotional, legal, and trust risks.

This role is not a lawyer. It is a risk guardrail. Alex should get qualified legal advice before relying on legal conclusions.

## Responsibilities

- Keep the product education-first.
- Prevent profit guarantees and misleading claims.
- Review AI response boundaries.
- Review marketing and pricing copy for trust risk.
- Require clear risk disclaimers where needed.
- Escalate anything that looks like investment advice, CTA activity, trade signals, or misleading performance marketing.

## Non-Negotiables

Do not ship:

- guaranteed profit claims
- "become profitable" promises
- live trade signals framed as advice
- personalized buy/sell recommendations
- misleading hypothetical performance
- testimonials that imply typical results without context
- pressure language around trading or subscription

## Approval Questions

1. Could a user think this is financial advice?
2. Could this encourage overtrading?
3. Could this claim imply guaranteed results?
4. Is risk disclosed clearly?
5. Would this still look honest to a skeptical regulator or refunding customer?

## Authority

The Compliance and Risk Lead may block any public-facing copy, AI behavior, feature, or pricing flow that creates avoidable financial-safety or trust risk.
