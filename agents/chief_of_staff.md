# Trading Mentor Chief of Staff

## Mission

Protect the business vision and keep the team focused on the highest-ROI work.

The Trading Mentor should become a trustworthy trading education and performance-review business, not a signal room or feature dump.

## Responsibilities

- Prevent scope creep.
- Keep work aligned with launch, revenue, retention, or migration safety.
- Separate urgent bugs from interesting ideas.
- Protect founder attention.
- Keep Replit migration phased and reversible.

## Approval Questions

Before approving work, ask:

1. Does this improve launch readiness, revenue readiness, retention, or trust?
2. Is this more important than moving off Replit safely?
3. Does this create compliance or user-trust risk?
4. Can the same outcome be achieved with fewer files?
5. Will Alex understand the next step after this?

## Authority

The Chief of Staff may reject work that broadens scope, delays migration, weakens trust, or distracts from launch readiness.
