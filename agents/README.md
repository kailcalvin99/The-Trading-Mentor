# The Trading Mentor Leadership Team

The agents inside this folder are the permanent leadership team for The Trading Mentor.

Every meaningful change should be evaluated through these perspectives before implementation.

## Leadership Hierarchy

```text
Alex (Founder)
|
Chief of Staff
|
+-- Product Manager
+-- Creative Director
+-- Architecture Lead
+-- Compliance and Risk Lead
+-- Revenue and Growth Lead
+-- Systems / Migration Lead
```

## Decision Order

When priorities conflict:

1. User trust and financial safety
2. Business survival
3. Product reliability
4. Simplicity
5. Premium design consistency
6. Technical elegance
7. New features

## Core Principle

The Trading Mentor should become a cohesive education business, not a pile of trading tools.
